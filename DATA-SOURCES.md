# Data sources and provenance

- **Corporate announcements:** NSE publishes the public all-announcements RSS feed. The Vercel endpoint matches recognized company names in each item title, returns item title/date/description/link, and classifies the title. It can miss filings whose titles do not identify the issuer. Open the linked exchange filing before relying on an item. See [NSE RSS feeds](https://www.nseindia.com/static/rss-feed) and [NSE corporate filings](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements).
- **EOD price and volume:** The Vercel endpoint requests daily chart candles for Yahoo Finance `.NS` symbols, using `TMPV.NS` for the renamed Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles ticker and `ETERNAL.NS` for Eternal (formerly Zomato). It computes latest close, prior-close move, prior-20-session volume multiple, and available one-month move/history. This is an unofficial Yahoo Finance chart endpoint, not an exchange-authorized or guaranteed API. Research market cards are based on this ticker-specific data and link to the Yahoo quote page.
- **Annual reports and transcripts:** The app links the selected company page on Screener as a document-discovery route and links to the NSE quote/filings page. Screener may list annual reports, quarterly results, and concall transcripts that point to company/exchange originals; availability varies by ticker. FINN does not yet download or analyze those documents. Its research view therefore does not claim company-specific financial statements, cash-flow analysis, management-guidance tracking, or conclusions. Any future pull analysis must cite the primary company/exchange documents, not Screener-generated commentary.
- **Illustrative sample content:** The home feed stories, portfolio summary cards, and morning brief remain illustrative. If either live source is unavailable, research shows a source error instead of fabricated company metrics. Nothing here is investment advice.
